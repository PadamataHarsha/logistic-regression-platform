IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Accuracy is the share of test examples classified correctly. Precision measures how often positive predictions were right; recall measures how many actual positive cases were found. F1 balances precision and recall.

These scores describe this held-out sample only. They do not guarantee performance on future visitors or different populations.
